Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

My Monkey Business ("the app") is an educational app for practising stock investing with pretend money, made by Cloudeation ("we", "us"). No real money is ever deposited, traded or withdrawn. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your choices.

What we collect

DataWhat it isWhy
AccountYour email address. If you sign in with a password, it's stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email (Apple may give a private relay address) and, if you share it, your name.To sign you in on any device.
ProfileA display name, which you can change any time in Account.Shown in the app and to members of circles you join.
Practice activityYour pretend cash balance, stocks held, practice trades and the reasons you write for them, reflections after selling, watchlist, lessons completed, discipline rule checks, XP, levels, badges, and pretend paychecks, bonuses, interest and dividends.To run your practice account, enforce the discipline rules and show your progress.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or any real bank, brokerage or payment information.

Circles — what other people can see

If you create or join a circle, its members can see your display name, gain in dollars and percent, Discipline Score, level and weekly streak. They can never see your holdings, trades, cash, reasons, reflections or watchlist. Messages you write in a circle's chat are visible to that circle's members. You can report a message or block someone from the chat, and leave a circle at any time. Reported messages may be hidden and reviewed.

Explanations ("Why did this move?")

When you ask why a stock moved, the stock symbol, its price change and public news headlines are sent to Anthropic to write the explanation. None of your personal information is sent.

Advertising

The app currently shows no ads and does not track you for advertising. If we add ads in a future version, we'll update this policy first, and where the law requires it the app will ask for your consent before any ad is requested.

Service providers

  • Supabase — hosts accounts and app data (United States).
  • Apple and Google — sign-in, if you choose them.
  • Finnhub and Twelve Data — public market data. They receive no personal information.
  • Anthropic — writes explanations from public market data.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share it except with these providers to run the app, or when required by law.

Legal basis (EEA / UK users)

We process account and practice data to provide the app you asked for (contract).

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account any time in Account → Delete account; this permanently removes your profile, practice trades, holdings, watchlist, progress, circle memberships and chat messages. If you can't open the app, see how to request deletion.

Your rights

You can access, correct (display name) or delete your data in the app. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request a copy of your data or object to processing — email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each person can only read their own account data. Circle information is limited to the fields listed above.

Children

My Monkey Business is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Not financial advice

Everything in the app, including explanations and lessons, is for education only and is not investment advice.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
