Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email support@cloudeation.net and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Is any of this real money? No. Everything is pretend money at real market prices, for learning.

Why was my trade blocked? The app has 7 discipline rules — for example $1,000 of buys a day and no stock above 20% of your portfolio. The message on the trade screen says which rule and when you can try again.

When do I get paid? A $100 paycheck arrives every Monday — open the app that week to collect it. Interest on cash and dividends are paid monthly.

How do circles work? Create a circle on the Progress tab and share the 6-character code. Members see each other's gain and Discipline Score, never holdings or trades.

Someone is being rude in a circle chat. Long-press their message and choose Report or Block. Messages reported twice are hidden for everyone. You can also email us — we review reports and remove people who break the rules. Undo blocks in Account → Unblock everyone.

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen tap "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a 6-digit code.

Delete your account

In the app: open Account → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your profile, practice trades, holdings, watchlist, progress, circle memberships and chat messages.

Without the app: email support@cloudeation.net from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete everything within 30 days and confirm by email. Nothing is kept afterwards.
