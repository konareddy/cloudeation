Pretend money · real prices

Learn to invest with discipline — before it's real money.

My Monkey Business gives you $10,000 of pretend money to buy and sell real US stocks at real prices. Built-in rules teach the habits good investors follow, and friends can compete in circles on who invests most wisely.

7 discipline rules

$1,000 a day, no stock over 20%, a reason for every buy, a pause before chasing hype — the habits that protect real savings.

Discipline Score

Earn XP, levels and badges for patience, diversification and reflection — not for lucky bets.

Paydays & interest

A $100 paycheck every week, interest on cash and dividends — just like a real investor building wealth over time.

Circles

Start a circle, share the code, and see who in your group invests with the most discipline. Friends see each other's gain and Discipline Score — never what anyone owns.

Bite-size lessons

Short lessons explain how the market works, and "Why did this move?" explains big price moves in plain English.

Available on iPhone and Android. Education only: no real money is deposited, traded or withdrawn, and nothing in the app is investment advice.
