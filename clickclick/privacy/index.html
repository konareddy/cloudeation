Privacy policy

ClickClick – Test Evidence Recorder, Chrome extension · Effective November 14, 2026

ClickClick records your browser test steps and turns them into a test evidence document. This policy explains how information is handled by the extension and when you contact Cloudeation Technologies for support.

Your recordings stay on your computer

Recordings, screenshots, videos, URLs and the values you type are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB and Chrome extension storage). No ClickClick account is required. We do not operate a server that receives your recordings, and the extension contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What is recorded

Only while a recording is running, and only in the browser window where you pressed Start, ClickClick records:

  • screenshots of the web page content (never the address bar, tabs, bookmarks or other browser interface);
  • the URL and title of each page you visit;
  • your actions: clicks, and values you enter in fields or select in dropdowns. Passwords and card- or one-time-code-like fields are masked;
  • optionally, a video of the tab, only if you turn video recording on.

The extension also stores your settings, such as the last test name and the screenshot mode.

Permissions

ClickClick asks for access to all websites because you choose which site to test. It uses that access only to take screenshots and note your actions during a recording you started. Tab capture is used only for the optional video recording.

Exports and sharing

Documents you export (Word, PDF, HTML) and videos are saved where you choose. Anyone who can access an exported file can read it, including the screenshots and URLs it contains. Share exports only with people and services you trust.

Retention and deletion

Recordings remain in your browser until you delete them from Past recordings or remove the extension. Removing the extension deletes its stored data; exported files are not affected.

ClickClick Pro licences

ClickClick is currently free. If a Pro licence is offered and you activate a licence key, the extension sends only the licence key and a device label (for example “ClickClick on macOS”) to our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, to check that the key is valid, about once a week. No recordings, URLs or screenshots are ever sent. Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy under its own privacy policy.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising, creditworthiness or any purpose other than producing your test evidence documents. Our use of information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Support correspondence

If you email kona@cloudeation.net, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them only to respond to your request. Please avoid sending test evidence that contains confidential data unless needed. You can ask us to delete support correspondence.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver and secure the page. It adds no analytics scripts, cookies or contact forms.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if our practices change. For privacy questions or requests, email kona@cloudeation.net.
